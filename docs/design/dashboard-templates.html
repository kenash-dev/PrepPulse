PrepPulse — design templates

Two parts share one capture engine and database: A tracks focus live during the current study session (taskbar, tray, alerts), and B shows overall progress against the plan. Everything runs on sample data for 26 Oct – 8 Nov 2026 with a mock interview date of 18 Dec. Hover any chart for details. Cards marked MVP are the suggested first cut.

A

Current session — live focus tracking

What you see while studying: start/stop, what has focus right now, every app open during the session, idle detection, and alerts when focus is lost. Driven by your focus and distraction lists. Lives in the taskbar and tray; never needs the dashboard open. A session ends when you stop it, or when the PC sleeps, restarts or shuts down.

The tray icon (bottom-right, by the clock) is a ring: how full it is = today's progress, its colour = focus state. Bar length and colour are separate signals — you can be at 77% of today's target and still be off-task right now.

Windows 11 tip: new tray icons start hidden behind the ^ overflow, and apps can't pin themselves. On first run PrepPulse shows a one-time tip and opens Settings › Personalization › Taskbar › Other system tray icons so you can switch it on (or drag the icon out of the overflow).

Focus-lost alert

MVP

Fires when, during a session: no keyboard or mouse input for 5 min, a distraction in focus for 2 min, or an app or site on neither list for 5 min. Each threshold is configurable. Built from: idle gaps, focus segments, your lists.

PPrepPulse · now

WhatsApp is on your distraction list (since 10:20). Last focus: leetcode.com · 15. 3Sum.

PPrepPulse · now
No input for 5 minutes

Still with it? Last focus: arxiv.org (15:20).

  • One alert per gap; no repeats until you're back on task.
  • "Break" snoozes alerts and pings when the break ends.
  • "I'm reading" marks the gap as prep. Focus-list entries marked "passive OK" (videos, PDFs) don't trigger the no-input alert.
  • No alerts while the PC is locked (the session pauses). Sleep, restart or shutdown ends the session.
  • If Windows Do Not Disturb hides the toast, the tray icon and taskbar still flash and the beep (if on) still plays.
