PrepPulse — dashboard design templates

Candidate graphs for an interview-prep tracker that records sessions (start/stop), the focused app and window, and pauses of 2+ minutes with no keyboard or mouse input. Every chart runs on sample data for 26 Oct – 8 Nov 2026 with a mock interview date of 18 Dec. Hover any chart for details. Cards marked MVP are the suggested first cut.
